## Design direction

Two flowing forms divided by a rising channel suggest smooth movement and seamless exchange. Electric blue and cyan accompany a deep-indigo geometric wordmark. The symbol retains its open channel in monochrome.

The brief's color values are art-direction targets; the raster output is not a calibrated color specification. This is a visual identity concept, not vector master artwork. The prompts requested distinct geometry and avoidance of existing tech brand marks; no trademark or similarity clearance was performed.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one polished brand identity presentation board for an original cross-platform file-sharing product.

Create a premium, beautifully resolved, minimal logo identity for the app named exactly "Glide" (capital G, lowercase l i d e). The product is fast, smooth, simple, trustworthy wireless file sharing across phones, tablets, desktops and browsers.

Design one distinctive proprietary symbol paired with an excellent wordmark. Symbol direction: a compact "glide channel", made from only two broad, softly curved flowing forms separated by one clear ascending negative-space passage. Slightly offset forms and controlled asymmetry convey smooth forward movement and seamless exchange. Rounded exterior transitions, gently tapering proportions and intentional blunt terminals. Make the silhouette sophisticated, balanced and memorable, with a generous interior channel that remains legible at tiny app-icon sizes. Resolve this as a carefully designed logo with strong simple geometry, not an illustration. No literal letter G, no generic loop, no arrowheads, no Wi-Fi arcs, no tiny accent pieces. The same exact symbol geometry must be repeated consistently everywhere.

Wordmark: text exactly "Glide", carefully kerned modern geometric sans serif, medium weight, premium, clean, readable, subtly softened and slightly futuristic. Upright lettering, not italic; no excessive customization. Dark deep indigo (#151B38). Make it feel professionally drawn and optically spaced. No slogan.

Color: symbol uses a restrained electric-blue (#375BEE) to cyan (#22CBE5) gradient. Flat vector-like edges; the gradient must be purely color, not lighting or dimensional volume. Indigo app icon background. Solid black and white versions preserve identical geometry and have no gradients.

Composition: one clean, spacious landscape brand identity board on a plain nearly white background. Sophisticated editorial spacing, excellent hierarchy, very generous margins, no clutter, no decorative background.
Upper half: the primary horizontal logo, large and prominent, symbol on left and "Glide" on right, optically aligned, elegant balanced spacing. A tiny understated neutral-gray section label reads "PRIMARY LOGO".
Lower half: three evenly spaced distinct presentations with ample breathing room. First: the colored standalone symbol, with small label "ICON". Second: the standalone "Glide" wordmark, label "WORDMARK". Third: an app icon concept, one flat deep-indigo rounded square containing the same centered blue/cyan symbol with generous safe padding, label "APP ICON". No smartphone or device mockup, no perspective.
Bottom: a slim, restrained monochrome proof row with a small black primary logo on white and a small white primary logo on a plain black rectangular swatch, plus tiny label "MONOCHROME". Keep these visually subordinate. The whole presentation must have ample whitespace, large enough logos to judge, exact consistent shapes, perfect text.

Style: high-end real startup identity, simple and scalable, elegant, calm, technologically advanced, exceptionally polished vector-style graphic design. Flat, not 3D. No shadows, no glow, no texture, no glass, no bevels, no metallic effects, no stock-art ornament.
Avoid: childish or playful shapes, bulky symbols, file/folder clipart, wireless/radar circles, paper planes, opposing arrows, infinity signs, swoosh checkmarks, generic stock-logo looks. Do not imitate SHAREit, Xender, AirDrop, Google, Dropbox, Microsoft, Slack, or other existing recognizable tech brand marks. Explore original geometry rather than modifying an existing logo. No extra words, no watermark, no legal/trademark symbols.
Render at high resolution with crisp edges and beautifully readable type.
