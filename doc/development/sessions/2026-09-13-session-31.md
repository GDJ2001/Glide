# Development Session Report: 2026-09-13-session-31

## 1. Objectives and Task Scope
- **Task:** `GLIDE-031` — Implement bounded orphan maintenance.
- **Milestone:** M5 — Background and recovery.
- **Priority:** P2.
- **Scope & Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Unique constrained WorkManager maintenance job (`glide_orphan_maintenance`) running periodically every 24 hours under `batteryNotLow`, `storageNotLow`, and `deviceIdle` constraints using `ExistingPeriodicWorkPolicy.KEEP`.
  - 24-hour orphan policy: safely purge unreferenced `.part` files in `files/receiving/` and cached sources in `cache/staging_sources/` and `cache/staged_sources/` older than 24 hours (`now - 24h`).
  - Active file protection: strictly exclude files belonging to active transfer sessions (`TransferSessionManager.getActiveSessionId()`) or referenced by active journal entries (`receiveJournalDao.getPendingEntries()`).
  - Immediate startup cleanup guarantee: startup reconciliation (`ProcessDeathReconciler`) ensures scheduler delay cannot lose verified files or leave startup inconsistent.
  - Storage unavailable & cancellation safety: graceful handling when storage is unmounted or inaccessible, and cooperative cancellation (`isStopped`) with immediate handle release.
  - History retention trimming: trim terminal history exceeding 10,000 sessions or 90 days (`HistoryStore.trimRetention`), preserving verified received files.
  - Automated tests: Active-vs-orphan, repeated worker (idempotency), storage unavailable, cancelled job, missing catalog audit, source cache purging.
