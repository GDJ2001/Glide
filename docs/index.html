Glide Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 14, 2026 • Version: 1.0.0

Glide ("the Application") is an open-source, local-only, zero-cloud peer-to-peer file transfer application developed for Android, Windows, Linux, and Apple (macOS, iOS, iPadOS). This Privacy Policy details our technical architecture and operational principles concerning your privacy when using Glide.

No User Accounts

Zero sign-up, registration, email, or identity requirements.

Zero Cloud Storage

Files travel directly from device to device over local radio.

Zero Trackers or Ads

No analytics SDKs, crash beacons, or ad networks included.

Strict Cryptographic Checks

Streaming SHA-256 verification and quarantine staging on all transfers.

1. Information Handled by Glide

1.1 File Content and Metadata

When you select files to send, Glide reads the filename, MIME type, file size, and computes a cryptographic SHA-256 hash strictly for payload integrity. This data is transmitted directly to the proximate peer device over an encrypted peer-to-peer channel.

  • In Transit: Direct device-to-device streaming is encrypted end-to-end using TLS 1.3 or Google Play Services Nearby Connections (TLS/DTLS). Files never transit third-party servers, cloud relays, or internet gateways.
  • On Device: Received payloads are initially quarantined in an app-private sandbox (Android/data/dev.glide.app/files/staging/) as .part files. Only after a byte-for-byte SHA-256 match is verified against the sender's manifest is the file committed. You can export verified files to your device's Downloads directory at any time.

1.2 Device Name and Cryptographic Identity

During local discovery, Glide broadcasts a customizable device display name (defaults to your device model, e.g., "Pixel 10") and an ephemeral local session token. This token is visible only to proximate devices searching for a transfer partner on the same local Wi-Fi or within Bluetooth range. Glide does not collect or transmit this identifier off-device.

2. Android Device Permissions and Purpose

Glide requests only the minimum runtime permissions necessary to negotiate local transfers:

Permission Platform Purpose
NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES Android 13+ (API 33+) Discovers and connects to nearby peer devices over Wi-Fi Direct without requesting device location. Declared with neverForLocation.
BLUETOOTH_SCAN, ADVERTISE, CONNECT Android 12+ (API 31+) Discovers proximate peer devices and performs initial connection negotiation. Declared with neverForLocation.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Android 12 & below Mandated by Android OS to scan for local Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth beacons. Glide never queries, records, or shares your physical GPS location.
CAMERA Android 8.0+ (API 26+) Strictly optional runtime permission used exclusively to scan invitation QR codes for local peer pairing. Video frames are analyzed in-memory and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted off-device. A manual numeric code or URI paste alternative is always available without camera access.
ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK Android 17+ (API 37+) Allows discovering and establishing direct encrypted TLS 1.3 sockets over the local Wi-Fi network.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Android 13+ (API 33+) Displays an ongoing transfer progress notification, allowing background transfer monitoring and cancellation.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE (connectedDevice) All Supported Maintains active device-to-device sockets alive when switching apps or locking the screen during high-throughput transfers. Terminates immediately upon completion or cancellation.

Glide never requests MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_CONTACTS, READ_PHONE_STATE, RECORD_AUDIO, or RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED.

3. Third-Party Services and Telemetry

  • Google Play Services (Nearby Connections): Used on Android for local Bluetooth discovery and Wi-Fi Direct handshakes. Google Play Services may collect standard diagnostic metrics per the Google Privacy Policy. Glide does not collect, receive, or store these diagnostics.
  • Zero Developer Telemetry: Glide contains no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, or third-party monitoring beacons.
  • Zero Advertising: Glide contains no advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.

4. User Data Control and Deletion

  • Transfer History: You can purge individual session items or all transfer records at any time in Settings > Transfer History.
  • Received Files: You can delete all locally received files or individual items directly from Settings > Storage.
  • Trusted Devices: You can inspect and revoke paired device cryptographic identities at any time in Settings > Trusted Devices.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling Glide immediately removes all app-private databases, quarantine caches, and unexported received files from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Glide is a general-purpose local file utility that does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13.
