## 1. Summary of Changes

Delivered **Milestone M19**, fulfilling Step 4 of the Glide Cross-Platform Path ([`CROSS_PLATFORM_PATH.md`](../../future/CROSS_PLATFORM_PATH.md)), defining the comprehensive architectural design, transport evaluation, cryptographic contracts, and platform sandbox rules for native Apple platform clients (iOS, iPadOS, and macOS):

1. **Architectural Decision Record Authored & Accepted ([`docs/adr/ADR-0020-apple-platforms-architecture.md`](../../adr/ADR-0020-apple-platforms-architecture.md)):**
   - Evaluated native transport options: MultipeerConnectivity (rejected: proprietary/Apple-only), CoreBluetooth (rejected: bandwidth ceiling ~100–200 KB/s), WebRTC (rejected: heavy dependency footprint >50MB), and Apple `Network.framework` over Local LAN with TLS 1.3 & Bonjour (adopted).
   - Enforced 100% offline, zero-cloud operations without reliance on APNs, iCloud, or external signaling relays.
   - Defined Short Authentication String (SAS) calculation (RFC 5869 HKDF-SHA256) and out-of-band QR code pairing (`glide://pair/v1#...`) with SPKI SHA-256 public key pinning.
   - Defined iOS/iPadOS foreground-first lifecycle with `beginBackgroundTask` grace periods (~30s) and orderly cancellation (`SYSTEM_CANCELLED`) avoiding forbidden background hacks.
   - Defined macOS companion lifecycle as a lightweight menu bar agent (`LSUIElement = true` / `NSStatusItem`).
   - Defined sandboxed quarantine pipeline (`.staging/<sessionId>/<itemId>.quarantine`) with single-pass `CryptoKit.SHA256` hashing and atomic commitment to Documents/Downloads/Photos Library.
   - Added ADR-0020 to authoritative index in [`docs/adr/README.md`](../../adr/README.md).

2. **Apple Platforms Technical Specification ([`docs/future/APPLE_PLATFORMS_SPECIFICATION.md`](../../future/APPLE_PLATFORMS_SPECIFICATION.md)):**
   - System architecture mapping: SwiftUI target for iOS/iPadOS, AppKit menu bar companion for macOS, and shared pure Swift package `GlideCoreKit`.
   - Wire protocol and framing mapping: `NWListener` on port 42424, `NWConnection` with TLS 1.3, `LanRecord` framing, and custom certificate verification block (`sec_protocol_options_set_verify_block`).
   - Bonjour discovery: `_glide._tcp.local.` service registration and `NWBrowser` monitoring with TXT records (`v`, `name`, `spki`, `presence`).
   - Sandbox entitlements and App Store privacy declarations: `NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`, `NSCameraUsageDescription`, `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`, and macOS `com.apple.security.app-sandbox`.
   - Detailed 6-phase implementation roadmap (A1 to A6).

3. **Roadmap Strategic Alignment ([`docs/future/CROSS_PLATFORM_PATH.md`](../../future/CROSS_PLATFORM_PATH.md)):**
   - Updated Step 4 to reference Accepted [ADR-0020](../../adr/ADR-0020-apple-platforms-architecture.md) and [`APPLE_PLATFORMS_SPECIFICATION.md`](../../future/APPLE_PLATFORMS_SPECIFICATION.md).
