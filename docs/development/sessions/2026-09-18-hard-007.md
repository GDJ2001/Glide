## Result

HARD-007 routes production Nearby sender and receiver connection/authentication/session startup through the foreground-service-owned `TransferRuntime` and proves that bulk transfer does not start before authentication and receiver consent.

## Production wiring delivered

- Sender selection persists a canonical `PreparedBatch` before discovery for both multi-file and retained legacy single-file paths.
- Discovered peer selection starts foreground-service-owned Nearby pairing.
- Receiver Nearby availability is started through `TransferService`, not a UI-owned connection scope.
- Discovery transport lifetime and authenticated session transport lifetime are separated.
- Session transports are bound to the active foreground-service scope and recreated when that scope changes.
- Exact SDK authentication digits surface through the production pairing state.
- Sender and receiver coordinators are created only after authenticated `ConnectionEvent.Connected`.
- Receiver advertising stops once an authenticated connection is established and on service/runtime teardown.
- Receiver protocol offers still require explicit offer consent before any bulk stream/staging starts.
- Pairing reject, peer loss, stale confirmation/action, exact cancel and revoked-source failure are fail-closed.
- Multi-file source mapping survives navigation/pairing and reaches the sender coordinator intact.

## Behavioral evidence

`Hard007NearbyProductionFlowTest` proves:

1. selected multi-file batches survive discovery/pairing and enter the coordinator intact;
2. no sender coordinator exists before authenticated `Connected`;
3. exact Nearby authentication digits are surfaced and confirmation is single-use;
4. stale peer confirmation is rejected;
5. pairing rejection and peer loss do not start transfer sessions;
6. receiver coordinator starts only after authenticated connection;
7. receiver offer remains in `AwaitingConsent` and produces zero staging/bulk work before consent;
8. stale offer actions are rejected and explicit offer rejection creates zero bulk/staging work;
9. stale session cancel is rejected while exact active-session cancel succeeds;
10. revoked source access after remote consent fails before a bulk stream is prepared or sent;
11. receiver advertising is stopped after pairing.

Additional regression coverage proves:

- `SelectionViewModelTest.prepareReadyFileForTransfer_storesCanonicalSingleFileBatch`
- `ProductionDiscoveryControllerTest.serviceSessionTransport_isRecreatedWhenForegroundServiceScopeChanges`
